# SIGNALFORGE 2027 — BUSINESS MODEL, UNIT ECONOMICS, UNICORN THESIS & FUNDING v1
**Status:** scenario analysis and commercial execution thesis; not a valuation, earnings claim, approved pricing, active token offer or investment solicitation. Date 2026-11-14.

## 1. Big company thesis
Most crypto dashboards sell information; trading bots sell transaction speed; general AI assistants sell generic answers. SignalForge intends to accumulate user-specific, source-linked market understanding that can be revisited, tested, automated and shared. The durable asset is an **evidence-and-workflow graph** connecting market change, source, user question, research thesis, simulation, user-authorized action and result. A thriving creator/community ecosystem can supply complementary analysis and widgets, compounding utility without SignalForge authoring everything.

Investment narrative: **SignalForge is a market-native AI operating system, where every research artifact improves the network's ability to explain changes and every builder integration creates new utility and distribution.**
The end goal is a large recurring-revenue, globally adopted intelligence/workflow platform—not token valuation as a surrogate for company value.

## 3. Gross margin by activity (unit model)
Define contribution for every Agent run:
**CM_run = collected user price/allocated subscription revenue - LLM spend - market-data spend - chain/network cost - payment fee - error/retry/support allowance - creator share.**
Let monthly active researchers A, paid conversion c, net realized monthly ARPU p; subscription revenue R = A*c*p. Do not use signups as A, list price as realized p, or checkout clicks as R. Variable inference and data costs scale with real usage, not number of website visitors.
Example $0.15 average true variable cost per Agent run and 20 paid runs/user = $3 direct model/data cost/member; add infrastructure, data licensing, support, fraud and refund overhead. These are illustrative sensitivity inputs, not measured SignalForge spend.

## 5. Growth math and constraints
Organic loop: useful free sourced card → shared to venue-native community → qualified first-use research → saved thesis/alert → later-day return → paid need → peer referrals. Acquisition rate and retention are distinct.
Critical tests:
- Does first-source-to-saved-thesis conversion occur without a signup wall?
- Does scheduled research create meaningful repeat use compared with spam alerts?
- Do users pay for workflows under realistic LLM/provider COGS?
- Does a third-party builder bring non-overlapping new activated users?
- Do token incentives improve qualified cohort return after rewards end?
Core growth metrics: WQRR; WAU/MAU; D1/D7/D30 source-qualified cohorts; Agent task completion; paid conversion; paid retention; CAC by actual source; COGS/run; MRR, refunds and contribution margin. Token claims, minted amounts, wallet count or trading volume are not equivalent to product-market fit.

## 6. Token economic integration boundaries
Token utility should be sourced from product demand: purchasable research/Agent credits, developer feature access, builder capacity and eligible governance/quest programs, not reflexive speculation.
Maintain explicit public distinction:
- Service revenue: actually consumed data/compute paid by users;
- token sale proceeds: financing under exact rights and terms;
- treasury token mark-to-market: unrealized asset value, not cash income;
- creator token allocations: potentially liabilities/dilution;
- trade/LP volume: neither revenue nor user retention.
No model should depend on future price appreciation to pay current hosting bills.

## 7. Premium positioning and 2027 outcome ambition
Differentiation against trading bots and dashboards:
1. Provenance and falsifiable research before execution;
2. persistent AI-native user thesis memory across market events;
3. cross-venue Polymarket/Hyperliquid/Solana context in one workspace;
