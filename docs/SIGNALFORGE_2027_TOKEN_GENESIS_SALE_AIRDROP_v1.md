# SIGNALFORGE 2027 — NATIVE TOKEN, GENESIS, QUESTS, AIRDROP, REFERRALS & POTENTIAL SALE v1
**Status: Proposed independent SignalForge tokenomics; no symbol, chain, contract, TGE, presale, allocation, market, price or claim is live or ratified.** Date 2026-11-14. Venture distinct from ARQANT AGC/token and Cairn CRN.

## 0. Thesis: economic utility, not fictional returns
SignalForge monetizes real market research and automations in fiat or stable payment first. A **future native token** can reduce friction to use metered Agent compute, researcher/builder products, access/capacity rights, optional community programs and rewards for verifiable contributions. Token holder does not own SignalForge equity, guaranteed revenue, automatic interest/yield, promised appreciation, or right to redemption in fiat. Do not market it as a guaranteed investment. Distinguish corporate treasury, protocol revenue, token flows, customer funds and creator payouts.

### Token utility candidates (subject to validation)
U1: Optional payment for eligible Agent run credits, automation quota and third-party research API usage with transparent rate/FX/provider conversion.
U2: Optional fee discount/rebate on supported premium tools; fiat subscription equally available, token not required.
U3: Access/capacity staking for tool publishers (possibly risk-bearing collateral only with appropriate rules; avoid advertising income for passive holding).
U4: Developer bonds/reputation assurance for published Skills, where legally and technically supported; non-transferable researcher reputation remains separate.
U5: Governance polling over discretionary ecosystem program parameters, not authority over customer wallets or venture ownership.
U6: Community incentives for source-reviewed research, verified tool creation, high-quality education and eligible active referring users.
Do NOT make trade-volume mining, price pumping, market-making promises, forced buys, or referral-only speculation the main use cases.

## 1. Two-stage Genesis strategy (ship Stage A immediately)
**Stage A — off-chain Signal Points, transferable value NONE:** user profile, quest board, proof ledger, anti-abuse, seasonal campaigns, public rules and transparent statuses. Points are non-cash, not exchangeable or redeemable for tokens by promise; future token allocation must have separate published conditions and eligibility. Credits/redeemable service vouchers require separate accounting from reputational XP.
**Stage B — proposed token and distribution:** finalize issuer/chain/contracts/vesting/allocations, security review, supported jurisdictions, actual issuer acceptance, claim interface, onchain transfer records, treasury and chain analytics. Explicitly communicate the precise cutoff/snapshot and accepted terms before eligible token entitlements exist.

## 3. Token launch design decisions before onchain commitment
Must set: issuer, ownership and IP, token name/ticker collision research, chain and bridge policy, distribution country matrix, token value/use rights, tax characterization, legal security/derivatives analysis, FINTRAC money movement role if any, contract code and audits, liquidity venue plan, transparent supply caps and burn/mint authorities, timelock/multisig, anti-bot, refund policy where applicable, custody segregation, privacy and sanctions controls, consumer risk disclosure, geo-eligibility if legally needed, pricing/dilution policy, vesting and treasury transparency. All *other* product development continues during these specific tasks. Do not mint, sell, claim or seed a mainnet pool without actual signers and verified parameters.

## 4. Token sale design: explicit branches, not one premature gate
**Distribution models to evaluate:** (A) genuinely consumptive access token with no investment pitch; (B) regulated/exempt financing where applicable; (C) qualified platform/issuer partner with defined roles; (D) no sale, product-funded development and optional token later.
No jurisdiction-wide assumption that calling a token "utility" or distributing it free removes securities obligations. For a real sale, obtain applicable jurisdiction determination and offer terms, connect real compliant payment/claim, and collect independent receipts. Do not use fake waitlists as revenue. If a geography is not eligible, do not market or execute sale there; continue nonfinancial research/community globally where lawful.
Token primary proceeds are financing receipts, not evidence that customers pay for useful research. Live liquidity/secondary trading and market value are distinct from cash profit.

## 5. Quest design: real utility and authenticity
Campaigns:
- Explorer: inspect one correctly sourced Polymarket, Hyperliquid, or Solana observation; free point eligibility on first verified event.
- Researcher: save one falsifiable thesis with source links and revisit on a later day.
- Reviewer: provide accurate, substantive counter-source or replication, subject to moderator acceptance.
- Builder: publish a functioning sourced widget or research Skill and pass test/review.
- Community: host useful permitted research thread or translate source documentation accurately.
- Referrer: invite a real new qualified participant who activates and returns; do not pay simply for social posting or installs.
- Customer: complete actual paid subscription/usage only if program terms permit; never promise investment gain.
Reject attempts: fake accounts, circular incentives, repeated tasks by one user, fabricated signals, self-referrals, coordinated wash orders, copied unsourced content, purchased traffic, bot-like bulk activity.
Each quest: id/version, target segment, proof event, fraud review, point score, per-user/day cap, season window, moderator appeal, immutable ledger entry, eligibility & jurisdiction rule, budget ceiling and public FAQ.

## 6. Referral engine
Unique campaign link and code per verified user; attribution through privacy-respecting short-lived landing ref and first-party confirmed account relationship; no unrelated project IDs. First award after authentic account and source-backed first research, second after a genuine different-day return or paid activation. Cap rewards and claw back abuse. Both inviter and invitee receive an actual in-product benefit such as finite free monitor credits/research export first; points may be layered, but no automatic financial token claim. Show referral state: invited / registered / first-use qualified / returned qualified / rewarded / rejected. Public disclosures for compensated links or token incentives required where applicable.
Example economics (planning, not approved price): free referral credit value ≤ observed gross contribution from activated/referred cohort. Anti-loss cap via verified unit margin and budget. Stop campaign only on documented negative margin or fraud; other growth proceeds.

## 7. Airdrop eligibility and actual distribution contract
Pre-token: "eligible activity may inform a future allocation, with no guaranteed token or cash benefit." Never show a balance as minted transferable token.
When onchain campaign approved: published token metadata, chain/address, eligibility rules, actual cutoff block/time, Merkle root or equivalent, exclusion appeals, allocated vs claimed ledger, vesting, wallet signature message bound to nonce/chain/campaign, replay protection, cap, snapshot reproducibility, unaudited/verified labels, unclaimed policy, onchain transaction receipts.
Avoid distributing to unrelated or unauthorized residents/regions; ensure claim contract cannot send more than the committed allocation. Do not demand wallet key or seed phrase.
Use independently verifiable claimant counts and executed token transactions; points != tokens and token claim != fiat realization.

## 8. Buy / sell / token utility accounting
Separate ledger categories:
A. fiat subscription and Stripe signed paid invoice;
B. token sale proceeds and refunds;
C. user payments for actual service consumption and model spend;
D. points (non-transferrable, zero redemption guarantee);
E. native token inventory locked/unlocked/transferred;
F. creator payouts and reward liabilities;
G. provider trading fees (if any and permitted);
H. LP principal, market value, fees and impairment.
Each item tracks asset/currency, payer/payee, provider reference, wallet/tx, chain, timestamp, settlement, fee and reversals. Never present project token sale as sales of SignalForge research subscriptions.

## 9. Stress tests before allocation is ratified
Build 24-month scenarios with 0/low/high users and realistic cost of AI calls, data licensing, creator rewards, fraud loss, refunds, market drawdowns, claimed unlocks and liquidity deterioration. Evaluate:
- utility demand with **zero token price appreciation**;
- compute rewards versus generated contribution margin;
- incentives cost per later-day returning researcher;
- founder/team unlock overhang, sales dilution, treasury runway;
- liquidity risk, concentrated holdings, and extreme volume cases;
- whether free users have a credible path to pay.
If model needs speculative inflows to fund ordinary provider costs, redesign emissions or pricing.
