## A. Market identity and exclusion
SignalForge is a **source-linked Web3 market-intelligence and research product** for actual Polymarket-event, Hyperliquid-perp and Solana-token researchers. Its charting/strategy/backtest tools are supporting product capabilities, not the primary ecosystem benchmark. ARQANT is an **independent token-native agent economy**, not SignalForge's token, funding pool, user database or internal spinout. This launch never assumes or promises a SignalForge token, airdrop, quote-side LP, trading market or ownership of ARQANT assets. If a future token thesis is ever proposed, it is a separately approved product decision, not an inherited ARQANT allocation.

## B. Community culture: evidence instead of fake alpha
**Belonging:** earn credibility by documenting market questions, source links, a falsifiable hypothesis and what actually changed. Keep discussion useful without paying or owning tokens. Community members may remain pseudonymous. Ban manipulated performance screenshots, hidden referral commissions, unsolicited financial promotions and unsupported profit claims.
Roles: Explorer → Researcher → Source Reviewer → Contributor → Community Moderator; these are **editorial/research roles**, not profit rights.
Real shareable content: Polymarket event/catalyst explainers, Hyperliquid raw funding/approximate OI methods and Solana mint/pool case studies. Signal Rooms can host member-generated hypotheses, not fake market performance.
Recurring culture: one daily data-source observation; three evidence-rich public research artifacts per week; one weekly "source/counter-thesis" review; one moderated builder issue/feature invitation. Count only native posts, reply receipts and actual user feedback.

## C. Web3-native audience and outreach
**ICP 1 / event researchers** (Polymarket live source, Kalshi still research only): direct useful entry `/web3#prediction`. Offer event volume, liquidity and time; publish a reproducible source-noted explainer to actual event-market forums and Polymarket builder conversations.

Outbound process: real current public question → one source-linked answer → genuine posted URL and permitted CTA → consent-respecting visit attribution → first qualifying research → signup if useful → D7 research return → paid membership only after delivered features → real settlement. Don't count drafted DMs as sent; never auto-post token shilling or synthetic research; separate a platform affiliate builder code from any claimed authorization (not presently established).

## D. Activation, educational gamification and return
**Free value:** inspect one real-source event/perp/token and understand provenance and limitations. Then optional watchlist, hypothesis note, research reply and return to a market. Keep membership for credible saved research, not gating open source evidence.
**Research progression (proposal only, not yet implemented):** "First Source", "3 Distinct Research Days", "Source Reviewer", "Contrarian Thesis", "Published Reproducible Research" recognitions. They have no financial value, token rights, airdrop, or trade-volume rewards; user-created research must pass moderation, not be awarded solely for clicks. 
**First-party instrumentation needed before claims:** user/session privacy-safe key, source mode, market lane, source observation time, event type; dedupe automation and test accounts. WQRR = unique eligible people who complete genuine source-based research on at least two distinct UTC days in 7d. Do not count upstream Polymarket/Hyperliquid market rows as SignalForge users.

## E. Revenue-first operating loop

The first qualified user research could be completed today (product is production-deployed); a real paid charge requires an actual consenting customer, valid checkout/entitlement, and independent processor settlement. No claim of current cash in this document.
Commercial experiment: (1) present finished free market source context; (2) collect specific researcher request to save/monitor/share a thesis; (3) show exactly implemented Pro/Quant benefit with real price; (4) test actual trial→paid→cancel/refund journey; (5) confirm one real settled charge and cost basis. **No user should be pushed toward a product feature that is only on the roadmap.**

## I. Distribution channels and cultures
Farcaster, Discord, Telegram, GitHub discussions, relevant Subreddits and educational X threads are **venues**, not automatically integrated distribution bots. Prefer offering one helpful factual response before a branded invitation. Treat private communities as permission-based. Do not pay for fake traffic or buy social engagement. If actual first-party outreach access is unavailable, prepare verified target references and permissioned drafts without saying they were sent.

The cross-project lesson exchange with ARQANT is limited to public best practices and independent technical education. There is **no direct shared wallet, user record, token, treasury, LP, chain deployment, product pricing or venture ownership**.

## J. Immediate decision rule
When source-backed first research is low, improve the real landing→first-source journey. When signup is high and return is low, find the absent repeat utility. When saved research is valuable but no one pays, test truthful Pro/Quant benefits and price/checkout friction. When market sources fail, repair provider/labeling while other channels continue. Company OS chooses the next bounded WorkPacket from source readback and records actual CI/deploy/retention and financial outcomes separately.
