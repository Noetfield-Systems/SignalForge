## North star and evidence
**WQRR = weekly qualified returning researchers**: eligible, genuine humans who perform two source-backed research actions on separate UTC dates within seven days. Status: **NOT YET MEASURED AS DEFINED**. A page load, upstream feed item, anonymous click or demo fallback is not a verified user research action.

Operational funnel: attributable venue/community arrival → source-backed first investigation → optional real account creation → research saved/returned on a later day → opted-in genuine paid subscription → independently reconciled settled cash minus real costs. Track three lane-specific cohorts; unknown instrumentation does not justify estimated numbers.

**First 14-day target hypotheses, NOT accomplishments:** 30 first qualified researchers, 8 substantive feedback reports, 6 second-day returning researchers, 10 verified public research contributions and one reconciled paid subscription payment. Use two measured 7-day cohorts for decisions, not speculative conversion claims.

## Direct distribution, not vanity growth
Event community: publish a useful market movement explanation with Polymarket API link. Perps: explain raw Hyperliquid funding and USD OI approximation accurately. Solana: demonstrate a real mint/pool lookup and how to avoid treating liquidity as a safety guarantee. Reply to existing requests for help, participate according to community norms and log exact native published URLs/response evidence. Unsent messages are not posts. Source-first free value precedes signup. Referrals and partners are hypotheses until implemented and consented.

## Living documentation / non-stale operations
Before every public source claim, check primary venue docs and actual current application behavior. Refresh official source contracts at least every 7 days and ICP/KPI hypotheses every 14 days. A historic memo can remain archived but cannot override current Founder goal. Never silently promote an old planned API into a shipped product. If evidence ages out, label it STALE/UNKNOWN and dispatch targeted repair while other independent lawful tasks continue.

The canonical operating chain is `Founder mission M20 → scoped WorkPacket (ICP, KPI, source, artifact) → existing Governor/Fabric → GitHub/CI → Railway readback → independent verifier → honest cohort/revenue measurement → next mission`. Company OS is internal, no extra queue or fabricated 100 active workers.
