How setups are scored

Explainable model · version 1

Confluence composition

Each strategy assigns weighted points for trend alignment, price structure, momentum, volatility and participation. A score is a setup-quality heuristic, not a calibrated probability of winning.

Trade construction

Entry is anchored to the completed signal candle, stop-loss is ATR-derived and three target tiers are calculated. A signal is invalidated when its protective level is crossed. No order is routed in this release.
