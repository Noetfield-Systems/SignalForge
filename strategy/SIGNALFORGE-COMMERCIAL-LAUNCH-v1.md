# SIGNALFORGE — Commercial Launch System and Brand Positioning
Version: 1.0 · 2026-11-11 · Status: operating plan, not evidence of commercial traction
Parent/operator: Noetfield Systems Inc. · Product: SignalForge · Mission: M20-SIGNALFORGE-COMMUNITY

## 1. Brand decision
**Category:** Onchain Research & Change Intelligence.
**Positioning:** Know what changed, why it matters to your research, and where the evidence came from.
**Core promise:** Paste a Solana mint → inspect source-linked pools and market conditions → preserve observations → compare changes → organize your own research.
**Brand line:** Evidence before execution.
**Do not claim:** financial returns, trading accuracy, a token "safety score", autonomous trading, live paid customers, automatic monitoring when there are only on-demand snapshots, or verified funds collected without Stripe readback.

SignalForge is a premium **workflow** for the curious, active onchain researcher, not another one-screen price dashboard. The product must beat the practical research journey, not claim superior data to aggregators with established infrastructure.

## 3. The three loops
### Acquisition: public token utility
- Landing /radar must work without login, cards and charts must declare source and observation time.
- Shared /tokens/solana/<mint> pages must load and explain incomplete data. Before indexing at scale, render meaningful original content server-side with a clear canonical URL; avoid empty programmatic SEO.
- Measure: qualified visit → valid lookup → completed report → shared URL → authenticated save.
- Content program: independent weekly Solana research explainers; illustrate methodology, not "buy this token".
- Target queries: "how to check Solana token liquidity", "Solana token pool research", "how to track token liquidity changes", and contract-specific research pages with actual unique observations.
- Distribution: creator education, relevant forums and Telegram research channels only with their permission; no fake endorsements or unsolicited bulk posts.

### Retention: persistent research
- R1 currently has public live lookup and source-linked research; R2 adds on-demand PostgreSQL observations and comparison.
- Next: signed-in token-mint watchlist, annotated research journal, user-controlled scheduled monitoring, source freshness checks, low-noise alerts, opt-in email/Telegram delivery, alert delivery history and deduplication.
- **Critical truth:** R2 observation history is generated by user lookups; it is not an unattended 24/7 tracker.
- Product test: at least 5 users independently explain what changed in one token and come back intentionally for a second research session.

### Revenue: paid recurring utility
- Free: token discovery, core source-linked report, read-only educational community.
- Pro: CAD 29/mo or CAD 290/year as proposed in existing source; pay only for delivered personal research features, never projected alerts.
- Quant: CAD 99/mo or CAD 990/year as proposed; sell only functioning advanced workflows and explicitly mark optional third-party model endpoints as unconfigured if unavailable.
- Keep 30-day no-card trial untouched for existing users; allow *explicit* paid upgrade during trial; external Stripe hosted payment must be initiated by the human user.
- Do not interpret plan selection, dashboard record, payment link click, or test charge as verified positive-margin revenue.
- Upgrade trigger: after a meaningful repeated research action, not before the first free lookup.
- Track revenue bridge: Stripe succeeded invoice/charge → signed webhook → exact customer mapping → entitlement → settled cash → refunds/fees → contribution margin.

## 5. Launch distribution plan (organic-first)
**Channel 1 — Search.** Create 8 original educational guides targeting contract-checking intent. Each must show real source citations, examples, unknowns, and a link to /radar; ship search metadata and analytics before scaling. No doorway pages or hollow auto-generated token pages.

**Channel 2 — Product sharing.** Each research result gets a permalink; add OG cards and a concise "observed vs unavailable" summary. Let users copy a direct link in one click. Attribution should be non-invasive and privacy-safe.

**Channel 3 — Web3 research communities.** Recruit 10-20 genuine users from independent researchers and community moderators. Run asynchronous product tests using their own token choices. Request permission before quoting feedback or making case studies. Never treat an outreach message as an acquired user.

**Channel 4 — Telegram.** Create a simple read-only bot/Mini App entry once the source service and alert consent are tested. Share original evidence cards; any digital sales inside Telegram follow the platform's payment requirements.

**Channel 5 — B2B micro-teams.** When 3 real research groups request joint monitoring, offer a paid pilot with group research logs and token watchlists. Do not prebuild a generic enterprise product.

## 6. Activation-to-payment instrumentation
Track privacy-safe, server-backed events with unique eligible humans:
- public_radar_lookup_valid; report_observed; public_report_shared
- research_saved; monitoring_rule_created; alert_delivered; alert_opened
- research_room_contribution
- checkout_redirect_initiated; signed_invoice_paid; refund_reconciled; entitlement_updated
Exclude local tests, synthetic emails and bots. Never infer cash from a UI event.

**Suggested decision indicators (targets, not actual results):**
- Discovery → useful report conversion
- Report → signup conversion
- Eligible seven-day repeat research sessions
- Monitor creation and alert effectiveness (once implemented)
- Matured trial → verified paid conversion
- Net collected contribution per paying customer
- Cost per distinct activated researcher

On fewer than 100 eligible users, display counts rather than confident population-rate claims.

## 7. Commercial rollout acceptance
P0: real Solana lookup and report, timestamped data, service reliability, explicit free vs paid proposition, working signup and pricing page, existing paid trial semantics preserved.
P1: on-demand history comparisons, source and timestamp shown, safe failure when storage is unavailable, verified CI, deploy and rollback.
P2: signed and idempotent Stripe webhook, customer/payment ledger readback, portal/cancel/refund, accepted low-value authorized transaction only; call collected cash verified only after actual account evidence.
P3: persistent token watchlist and scheduled alerts with permission and opt-out.
P4: attribution, organic launch experiment, structured user feedback and 7-day retention observation.

Ship functionality in small production-reviewed releases instead of postponing free utility until every optional feature is complete.
